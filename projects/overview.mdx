<ProjectCard
  title="4-Axis Foam Cutting CNC"
  link="./foam-cutting-cnc/intro"
  imageUrl="/img/placeholder-cnc.jpg"
>
  A scratch-built, 4-axis hot-wire CNC machine designed for cutting complex
  shapes out of foam, such as model aircraft wings. The entire project, from the
  mechanical design and 3D-printed parts to the firmware and control software,
  was developed from the ground up. **Key Technologies:** **Microcontroller:**
  ATmega328P with custom Marlin-based firmware **Control System:** GRBL,
  Universal Gcode Sender (UGS) **Hardware:** NEMA 17 stepper motors, custom
  3D-printed components **Programming:** C++ (Firmware), Python (G-code scripts)
</ProjectCard>

<ProjectCard
  title="High-Precision Optical Encoder"
  link="./optical-encoder/intro"
  imageUrl="/img/placeholder-encoder.jpg"
>
  This project involved designing and building a high-resolution quadrature optical encoder to measure rotational speed and position with high accuracy. It features a custom-designed encoder disk, an infrared emitter/detector pair, and signal conditioning circuitry to produce clean, reliable digital signals.

_ **Key Technologies:**
_ **Microcontroller:** STM32F103 ("Blue Pill")
_ **Electronics:** Schmitt trigger for signal debouncing, comparator circuits
_ **Design:** Custom PCB design (KiCad), laser-cut acrylic
\_ **Programming:** C (Interrupt-driven pulse counting), Python (Data logging)

  </ProjectCard>
